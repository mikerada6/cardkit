## Scope
Implement the generic `Hand` per [data-model.md](../docs/data-model.md)
§Value Objects vs. Entities (Hand is a mutable domain object) and
§Relationships & Ownership. Deliver in `core.hand`: `Hand` — an ordered
collection of `Card`s with size/duplicate invariants, cards added/removed as
play proceeds (mutation confined to lifecycle methods). This is the base that
`PokerHand` and `BlackjackHand` build on in `games` (later stories); keep it
game-agnostic — no scoring, no variant knowledge. Enforce the boundary rules
from [conventions.md](../docs/conventions.md) §Immutability & Null Policy
(reject nulls; never return `null` collections — return empty). Invalid hand
construction/duplicate insertion throws a `core.error` domain exception. Lands
in `core.hand`.
